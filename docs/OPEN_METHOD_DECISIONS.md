# Open method and experiment decisions

No decision in this document has been silently frozen in code. Stage 0 contains no proposed-method implementation. A run that depends on an open row is blocked until the project owner supplies an exact choice and it is copied, with equations and edge cases, into a hashed `docs/PROPOSED_METHOD_FROZEN.md` or an AdvPO protocol freeze.

## Proposed method freeze table

### A. Exact pairwise geometry — OPEN

Choose the matrix exactly. Is it

`V = lambda I + sum_i d_i d_i^T`, with `d_i = e(x_i,y_i^chosen)-e(x_i,y_i^rejected)`,

or a Bradley–Terry/Fisher-weighted sum? Freeze the numerical regularizer, feature dtype/normalization, intercept treatment, accumulation precision, and whether geometry uses `D_rm_train`, `D_cal`, or a disjoint union. One vector per preference pair is mandatory; no accidental all-pairs construction.

Blocks: pairwise geometry and every proposed uncertainty calculation.

### B. Nonconformity score — OPEN

Give the scalar formula and orientation. Specify whether it predicts signed or absolute reward error, preference-margin error, or a parameter residual; what label it consumes; how `V` enters; how ties/zero denominators are handled; and whether any score-scale alignment is fit on calibration data.

Blocks: static/adaptive conformal calibration and any claimed coverage.

### C. Policy-shift adaptation rule — OPEN

Specify the exact mapping from allowed current/past rollout/reference feature differences to calibration weights or neighborhoods: importance/density ratio, kernel, nearest neighbors, or another estimator. Freeze training data, fitting loss, cross-fitting, clipping, normalization, bandwidth/hyperparameters, and out-of-support behavior. Current/future gold labels are forbidden.

Blocks: `q_alpha,t` and proposed-adaptive.

### D. Weighted quantile and guarantee — OPEN

Freeze target `alpha`, exact finite-sample index/correction, weight normalization, test-point weight (if any), deterministic ties, zero-total-weight fallback, clipping, and the assumptions/level of any marginal or weighted coverage statement. Do not claim ordinary exchangeable conformal coverage under policy shift unless the assumptions actually justify it.

Blocks: all calibrated uncertainty values and coverage claims.

### E. Update cadence and memory — OPEN

Choose update timing (rollout collection, optimizer step, or every `k` steps), current batch versus rolling/fixed window, window size/decay, warm-up, distributed synchronization, checkpoint contents, and whether the current sample may influence the quantile used to score itself.

Blocks: adaptive lifecycle, compute budget, checkpoint/resume.

### F. Proposed robust policy objective — OPEN

Specify how the calibrated quantity alters optimization: sample-wise pessimistic reward, an AdvPO-style batch confidence radius, constraint, gate/abstention, or another formula. Freeze reference centering, signs, coefficients, units/scale alignment, placement relative to clipping/KL, and near-zero edge cases.

Blocks: proposed reward adapter and all proposed policy runs.

### G. Reference responses — OPEN

Confirm whether Coste AlpacaFarm prompts use one frozen-SFT generation per prompt (recommended in `AGENTS.md`) or another permitted source. Freeze policy/tokenizer revision, prompt formatting, seed, max tokens, EOS behavior, decoding (`do_sample`, temperature, top-p/top-k), number of references, cache key, and behavior when a prompt changes. References must not be regenerated by the evolving PPO policy.

Blocks: AdvPO Eq. (9), proposed reference-centered objectives, and fair method comparison.

### H. Primary experiment track — OPEN

Confirm the recommended Coste-native primary track (clean proxy labels, beta 0 stress, PPO/AdvPO/proposed-static/proposed-adaptive) or choose 25% Coste noise / 30% AdvPO-style noise. Freeze the label-flip seed/manifest, beta schedule, paper-sized versus code-sized rollouts, step budget, evaluation cadence, and whether a separate AdvPO-protocol track is secondary. Never mix tracks in one curve.

Blocks: full-run configs and statistical seed budget.

## AdvPO adaptation decisions exposed by the audit

These are not proposed conformal equations, but the paper does not specify them sufficiently for a unique Coste implementation.

### I. Eq. (4) geometry regularization — OPEN

The paper specifies `M_D = lambda I + sum e e^T` but no numerical `lambda`. Freeze its value/units, accumulation set, dtype, feature normalization (normally none unless explicitly an adaptation), and factorization jitter policy. Report any jitter separately from scientific `lambda`.

Blocks: CI diagnostics and Eq. (9).

### J. Finite/distributed estimator for Eq. (9) `g` — OPEN

The paper uses expectations. Choose an exact finite estimator: complete global rollout pool paired by prompt with cached references is the natural interpretation, but it is not declared by the paper. Specify weighting, duplicated prompts, variable per-rank counts, incomplete final pools, all-reduce precision, and whether `g` is recomputed per rollout collection or uses history.

The current callback sees one gathered chunk (two samples in the checked one-rank config), not the complete four-rollout pool. Selecting callback-chunk statistics would be a documented approximation.

Blocks: faithful Eq. (9) reward and distributed implementation.

### K. Zero-displacement and small-`B` convention — OPEN

Eq. (9)’s `lambda_star=sqrt((g^T M_D^-1 g)/B)` is undefined when the quadratic form is zero. Freeze an algebraically stable implementation of the aggregate penalty and the per-sample/reference adjusted rewards near zero, including tolerance and behavior for `B=0`. The mandatory `B→0` test cannot be written until this convention is explicit.

Blocks: numerically safe Eq. (9).

### L. AdvPO reward rescaling — OPEN

The appendix says to disable running-standard-deviation scaling and multiply the post-subtraction reward by a factor described as a ratio of running means. It does not unambiguously state ratio direction, signed/absolute means, update order, initialization, epsilon, or distributed aggregation. Choose a precise state machine or explicitly omit rescaling as a labeled adaptation. Do not infer it from the existing Coste `scale_reward=false` flag; Coste still clips rewards to ±10.

Blocks: paper-faithful reward scale and comparisons across `B`.

### M. AdvPO `B` and checkpoint-selection protocol — OPEN

Confirm whether to use paper grid `B in {1,5,10,15}`, how to choose checkpoints without gold leakage, and which validation reward/uncertainty stability criterion is permitted. Freeze selection before looking at gold trajectories. The paper’s description does not give a fully executable selection rule.

Blocks: Section 5.2 selection and final reporting.

### N. Section 5.1 “reward difference” metric — OPEN

The paper reports correlation with “reward difference” without fully defining signed versus absolute gap or scale alignment. Predeclare raw signed gap, absolute gap, and any calibration-only affine alignment as distinct controlled metrics; identify which is primary. All estimators must be evaluated on identical stored samples from standard PPO runs.

Blocks: one primary correlation claim, but not collection of raw scores.

## Baseline engineering decisions requiring validation

These do not authorize method invention, yet must be resolved before Gate 1.

### O. Offline gold formatting/loading — OPEN pending fixture evidence

The checked YAML sets `is_alpacafarm_rm=false`, while the Coste README says true. The specialized Alpaca formatter has a missing f-string and currently emits literal placeholders. Freeze the expected serialized prompt using pinned AlpacaFarm fixtures, correct only the proven path, and demonstrate equality to the gold model’s training/evaluation template. Gold stays in a separate process.

### P. KL field migration — OPEN pending metric tests

Keep historical `policy/kl` under an explicit legacy name. Add a new field for response-only, sequence-summed/sample-mean `0.5*log_ratio^2` with global reduction and a separate name for the k3-like estimator. Decide schema version and downstream migration; do not overwrite old JSON semantics.

### Q. Dataset split manifest — RESOLVED for `coste_split_v1`

Owner decision on 2026-08-24: keep the two Coste source datasets separate. Hash-partition the pinned preference dataset's original `train` pool as 90% `D_rm_train`, 5% `D_rm_val`, and 5% `D_cal`. Hash-partition AlpacaFarm's pinned `unlabeled` pool as 80% `D_rl_train_prompts`, 10% `D_rl_val_prompts`, and 10% `D_rl_test_prompts`. Preserve each source's original validation split as an external evaluation split rather than moving it into training.

Coste-native overlap resolution on 2026-08-25: the two source datasets share
their underlying AlpacaFarm prompt universe by construction. The primary
`coste_split_v1` track permits this cross-source prompt reuse and records total
and RM-role/PPO-role overlap counts in the split manifest. Disjointness remains
mandatory within RM roles and within PPO roles. Consequently,
`D_rl_test_prompts` is held out from PPO training/model selection, but is not a
globally prompt-novel test relative to proxy-RM fitting. Any globally
prompt-exclusive variant is a separate named protocol and must not be pooled
with Coste-native results.

Implementation requirements are frozen as deterministic content-derived record/prompt IDs, seed `coste-split-v1-2026-08-24`, largest-remainder exact quotas, prompt-group assignment, strict within-track disjointness, a cross-source prompt-overlap audit, identical manifests across methods/seeds, and local payload/ID hashes. This resolves allocation only; it does not resolve any conformal equation in Section 12 of `AGENTS.md`.

## Decision-record template

For every resolved row, record:

- owner and decision date;
- exact equation/pseudocode and all constants;
- data roles and allowed information;
- distributed/batch semantics;
- numerical edge cases;
- state/checkpoint semantics;
- computational budget;
- required unit/synthetic/end-to-end tests;
- whether it is original AdvPO, original Coste, a controlled adaptation, or the new proposal.

A verbal choice or config value alone is insufficient when it leaves any of those behaviors ambiguous.
